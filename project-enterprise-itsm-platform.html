Enterprise ITSM platform

Lead architecture and technical ownership of the ITSM platform that runs service management for thousands of users across a national utility.

Client
National energy utility, Saudi Arabia
Scope
Architecture, platform ownership, integration, operations
Period
2013 – present
Status
Live
The challenge

A national utility depends on its ITSM platform for every incident, change and service request across the organization. The platform had to stay available, stay in step with SAP and Active Directory, and keep evolving through upgrades without disrupting operations.

The solution

Platform architecture

Designed and owned the platform across Incident, Problem, Change, Release and Service Request processes.

Enterprise integration

Built and maintained integrations with SAP, Active Directory and enterprise applications using AR System workflows and APIs.

High availability & DR

Implemented high availability and disaster recovery for ITSM and reporting components.

Upgrades & tuning

Led platform upgrades, performance tuning and environment optimization.

Knowledge transfer

Delivered training and documentation, and handed knowledge over to internal teams.
